The extension is a ready-made build that talks to the hosted API. Installing it is downloading a zip and loading it; there is nothing to run and nothing to configure.

## Chrome, Edge, Brave

1. Download [rate-my-ott-chrome.zip](https://github.com/nrjdalal/rate-my-ott/releases/latest/download/rate-my-ott-chrome.zip) and unzip it somewhere it can stay (the browser reads the folder every time it starts).
2. Open `chrome://extensions` (`edge://extensions`, `brave://extensions`), turn on **Developer mode** in the corner, click **Load unpacked**, and pick the unzipped folder.
3. Open or reload a Netflix tab.

Chrome installs extensions from outside its Web Store only this way; the "Developer mode" switch is Chrome's name for it, not a sign that anything else is needed.

## Firefox

1. Download [rate-my-ott-firefox.zip](https://github.com/nrjdalal/rate-my-ott/releases/latest/download/rate-my-ott-firefox.zip) and unzip it.
2. Open `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox`, click **Load Temporary Add-on**, and pick the `manifest.json` inside the folder.

Firefox keeps a temporary add-on until it closes; a signed build that stays installed is the next step.

## What you will see

Every title card gets a small yellow IMDb score in its top-right corner. The preview that opens when you hover a card shows "IMDb 5.4" beside its duration; the hero at the top of a page shows "• IMDb 8.5" in its metadata line; opening a title adds an "IMDb:" row with the score and vote count beside the cast and genres, linking to the IMDb page, and its "More Like This" cards get a badge too. A title with no score is either too new to have one, not on IMDb under that name, or a name several titles share that the extension refuses to guess between: a wrong score is worse than none. Search results stay bare, since Netflix states no year for them and a name alone is no proof (its 2026 Mexican "Lovesick" is not the British series).

## Options

The popup switches the whole extension, or just the card badges, off and on. The options page (from the popup) has one more setting: **Dim low-rated titles** fades the artwork of any title rated below a threshold you pick (off by default), so the eye skips it without anything being hidden or moved; a title without a score is never dimmed, and the badge stays either way.

## Updating

A new release is a new zip: unzip it over the old folder (or into a new one and load that), then click the reload icon on the extension's card. The [releases page](https://github.com/nrjdalal/rate-my-ott/releases) lists what changed.

## If nothing shows

Click the extension's icon. The popup says whether the API is reachable and how fresh the IMDb index is ("IMDb index: 619.2K titles, refreshed 2 hours ago"); "not built yet" or "API unreachable" names the cause. It also lists what the current Netflix tab asked about: how many titles got a score, and the ones that did not, grouped by why (not rated yet on IMDb, several IMDb titles share the name, not on IMDb under that name, namesakes of other years, no year stated). Both switches on, a reload of the Netflix tab, and a quit-and-reopen of the browser (which clears the extension's session memory) cover everything else. The extension sends only the title names Netflix shows, and nothing about you; see [Privacy](/docs/privacy).
